# Changelog

All notable changes to Sailor will be documented in this file.

## [0.1.0.0] - 2026-04-10

### Added
- Complete Sailor prototype: financial therapy app for solopreneurs
- Business expense review with swipe-to-categorize (High ROI / No ROI / Unsure)
- Personal expense review with swipe-to-categorize (Essential / Meaningful / Mismatch)
- Three-step review flow: classify → rate (1-4 stars) → clarify (ROI type or reason)
- Dashboard with three tabs: Overview, Personal, Business
- DoorDash-style horizontal scroll lanes for insights, quick wins, and action items
- Spend Score arc gauge with white dot indicator
- Financial health ratios (Income to Spend, Savings to Spend) with status badges
- Joy Spend Score for personal spending intent tracking
- ROI Optimization Score for business expense efficiency
- Spending Intent grid (Growth, Connection, Play, Health) with ratings
- Quick Wins section surfacing mismatch transactions to cancel or cut
- Insights drill-down pages for business and personal buckets
- Unsure micro-audit: re-review unsure transactions with same swipe flow
- Cashflow recategorization swipe flow
- Tax calculator with percentage slider
- CSV upload for transaction import
- Account management (add, link, view balances)
- Bucket system for financial goal tracking
- Tasks page with checkboxes and manual task support
- Month picker for filtering dashboard by period
- Score debug view with expandable per-transaction detail
- Spending score algorithm with 3-pillar weighting (income ratio, review quality, runway)

### Changed
- Dashboard expenses grouped by review bucket instead of category
- Rating scale normalized to 1-4 (from 1-10)
- Insights renamed to Patterns in navigation
- Grayscale-only design system during founder testing phase
